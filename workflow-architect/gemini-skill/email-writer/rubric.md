# Self-check rubric (score 1–5; fix anything below 4 before replying)
1. **Purpose clarity:** 5 = the first two lines tell the reader why this is for them and what's needed.
2. **Reader fit:** 5 = framed around the reader's priorities and relationship, and follows the right type playbook (`email-types.md`).
3. **Ask and next step:** 5 = one explicit ask with who, what and when, easy to answer. For replies, every question in the thread is answered in order.
4. **Concision and skimmability:** 5 = within the length range, short paragraphs, bullets for 3+ items, no banned filler, and a specific 2–7 word subject.
5. **Style adherence:** 5 = every requested control (tone, length, format, language, voice sample, notes) is fully followed.
6. **Honesty:** 5 = nothing invented, no commitments the user didn't make, and gaps flagged with [NEEDS]. Any fabrication = 1, so rewrite.
