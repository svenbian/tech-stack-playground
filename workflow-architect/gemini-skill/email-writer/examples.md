## Anti-example (what not to do)
**Subject:** Quick question!!
Hi there, I hope this email finds you well! I just wanted to reach out to touch base regarding the thing we discussed and see if you might possibly have any thoughts at your earliest convenience…
*Why it fails:* the point is buried, the subject is vague, it's all filler, and there's no clear ask or deadline.
